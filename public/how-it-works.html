How it works

Seven steps from a client's brief to a prepared candidate. The first happens once per client; the rest repeat with every submission.

Once per client

  1. 1Write down how the client hires

    Open Client knowledge and file what you know about the department: who interviews, what they ask, what has sunk a submission before, what they actually care about. Every pack for that client is built from this note, so the more you file, the better the packs get. You write it once and add to it as you learn.

Every submission

  1. 2Pick the client, add the brief and the CV

    On the Submission pack screen, choose the client, then paste the brief and the candidate's CV, or drop them in as PDF or Word files. Nothing you paste is stored: the CV and the brief live on your screen and nowhere else.

    On this demo, Load a worked example fills both boxes for you.

  2. 3Generate, and leave the page open

    One press. The engine writes the pack and checks every claim in it word for word against the CV and your client note. It takes a minute or two, and the page shows an honest clock while you wait.

  3. 4Check the claims

    Every line of the finished pack carries a mark saying where it came from. Lines drawn from the CV or from your note arrive checked; anything unverified is yours to confirm or cut before a client sees it.

    Eight years reporting CT and MRI across two NHS trusts. CV

    Comfortable with the on call pattern this department runs. Client note

    Holds a full UK driving licence. Unverified

  4. 5Copy the pack into your email

    One press puts the whole pack on your clipboard, formatted. Paste it into the email you were already writing to the client and send. That is the submission done.

Once the interview is booked

  1. 6Send the candidate their prep

    Enter the interview date and the candidate's email address. They get a private preparation page of their own, built from the brief, their CV, and only the parts of your client note you ticked as shareable. It never sends the pack. The candidate walks in knowing what the client will ask.

    The candidate's side of this demo opens from the top bar.

  2. 7Let the boards keep watch

    Three screens carry the follow-up so you do not have to.

    • Prep sent shows how many candidates opened their prep, and nothing about what they did inside.
    • Bookings emails you fourteen days before a booking runs out, in time to extend it or redeploy the candidate.
    • Compliance tracks what every candidate still owes you and what is about to expire. It keeps the reference number and the date, never the document.

From the second role onward the flow starts at step 2. The note is already written, and each pack you check shows you another line worth adding to it.
