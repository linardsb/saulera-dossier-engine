Prep sent

How many candidates we sent prep to, and how many opened it. Nothing about what any candidate did.

  1. 1Add the client and write its note. Start on the client knowledge screen. Add each client you work with, then write down how they hire: their stages, who interviews, why past candidates were turned down. That note is what makes every pack for the client better than a CV summary.
  2. 2Paste the brief and the CV. On the submission pack screen, pick the client in the list, then paste the client's brief and the candidate's CV into the two boxes, or drop in the files. Nothing you paste is stored: leave the page and it is gone.
  3. 3Generate the pack. Press Generate and leave the page open. Writing takes a minute or two because every claim in the pack is checked word for word against the CV and your note before you see it.
  4. 4Check it, then copy it. Every line in the finished pack shows where it came from. Anything marked unverified has no source, so confirm it or take it out before the pack leaves you. Then press Copy and paste the pack into your email or your ATS.
  5. 5Send the candidate their prep. When the client books an interview, enter the date and the candidate's email and press Send. The candidate gets a private page to prepare on, built only from what you ticked as shareable. This screen shows how many were sent and opened.
