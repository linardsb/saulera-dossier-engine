Interview prep

Your data on this portal

Plain answers: what we hold about you, why we hold it, when it is deleted, and how to delete it sooner.

What this is

This portal is a private space to prepare for your interview. Nothing you do here reaches the recruiter: your practice answers, your ratings and your notes stay between you and this portal.

Who holds your data

The recruitment agency that invited you is responsible for your data. In legal terms it is the data controller. The operator of this portal processes your data on the agency's behalf. Three services run underneath: Cloudflare hosts the portal and its database, Anthropic provides the model that builds your practice questions, and Resend delivers the invite email.

What we hold and why

What we hold Why we hold it When it is deleted
The job description, notes about the role, and your CV To build your practice sessions around the actual role 30 days after your interview date
Your practice attempts, ratings and notes To pick your next question and show you real progress 30 days after your interview date
Whether your invite was sent and opened So your recruiter knows the invite reached you 30 days after your interview date; anonymous totals remain

Your recruiter sees whether the invite was sent and opened. That is all. Nothing you practise, type or rate is ever shown to them.

Why we are allowed to hold it

The lawful basis is legitimate interests: you accepted an interview, and the agency that arranged it has a genuine interest in helping you arrive well prepared. If you want the full legal detail, ask the agency that invited you for its privacy information.

When it disappears

Everything in the table above is deleted automatically 30 days after your interview date. The deletion is permanent: there is no archive and no copy kept.

Delete it now

The delete control in your prep portal removes everything immediately. It covers the same data as the automatic deletion: the role details, your CV, and every practice attempt. You do not have to give a reason, and nobody is told you pressed it.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data held about you, have it corrected, or have it erased. Contact the agency that invited you; it answers data requests. If you are unhappy with the answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
