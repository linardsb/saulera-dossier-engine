# SYNTHETIC — agency's client-knowledge note

*Fabricated for spike #2. This stands in for the note the agency would keep per client —
architecture §4: "That note is the product. The generation is the cheap part."*

**Client:** Ashdown Park Community Healthcare

## Their process

Two stages. First a 30-minute conversation with the Clinical Services Manager, which is
mostly about whether you can hold your nerve alone in someone's house. Second stage is a
panel: the Clinical Services Manager, a senior district nurse, and someone from
governance.

Second stage always includes a scenario. The last two were a deteriorating patient found
during a solo visit, and a family disagreeing with a DNACPR decision. They are testing
escalation and documentation, not clinical knowledge.

## What they actually care about

The Clinical Services Manager has said twice that clinical skill is the easy part and
what they cannot train is comfort with autonomy. They ask about the last time you made a
call on your own with nobody to check it with.

Governance sits in on the second stage because they had a documentation issue two years
ago. Anything that shows careful record-keeping lands well.

## Why candidates have been turned down

- Feb 2026: strong acute nurse, no community experience. Turned down on the scenario —
  wanted to escalate everything rather than assess first.
- Nov 2025: good community experience but was vague about documentation. Governance
  blocked it.

## Practical

They move fast once they like someone: offer within a week of second stage. They will
not wait longer than three weeks for a notice period without a conversation.
