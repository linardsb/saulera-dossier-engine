**Client:** Ashdown Park Community Healthcare (private provider, two sites in West Sussex)
**Role:** Band 6 equivalent — Senior Community Staff Nurse, permanent
**Reporting to:** Clinical Services Manager
**Base:** East Grinstead, with a caseload across the Crawley corridor

## What we need

Caseload of ~25 patients in their own homes: complex wound care, catheter management,
end-of-life support, and IV therapy in the community. Working autonomously most days,
with weekly MDT.

Essential:
- NMC registration, no restrictions
- At least three years post-registration, at least one of them in a community or district
  nursing setting
- Independent wound assessment and management, including compression bandaging
- Comfortable lone working with a rural caseload; full UK driving licence and own car
- Experience of end-of-life care and syringe driver management

Desirable:
- Non-medical prescribing (V300), or willingness to start it within 12 months
- Experience with SystmOne
- Mentorship or preceptorship of junior staff

## What we've been struggling with

Two previous hires came from acute wards and found the autonomy hard. We would rather
have someone with less clinical breadth who is genuinely used to working alone in
people's homes than a strong ward nurse who has never done it.
